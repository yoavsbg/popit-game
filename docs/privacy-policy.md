# Pop It: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pop It ("the app") is a casual bubble-popping game published by Yoav Sabag.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

- **No account.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- **No network use.** The game works fully offline and does not connect to any server.
- **No permissions.** The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, best times, sound on/off and chosen bubble theme) is saved in a small file
on your device so the game can remember it. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Children

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out and no chat, and it collects no data, so it is suitable
for all ages.
