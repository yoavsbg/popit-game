How it works

Three rules. Endless retries.

  1. Find the rings

    Every level highlights a few bubbles with a ring. Those are your targets.

  2. Tap fast, tap right

    A tap on a plain bubble adds 1.000 seconds to your time. Clear every target to finish.

  3. Beat your best

    Your best time is saved for each level, so there's always a faster run to chase.
