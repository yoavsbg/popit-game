Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pop It ("the app") is a casual bubble-popping game published by Yoav Sabag. The short version: the app collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No network use. The game works fully offline and does not connect to any server.
  • No permissions. The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, best times, sound on/off and chosen bubble theme) is saved in a small file on your device so the game can remember it. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

This website

This website (not the app) uses Counter.dev for anonymous visit statistics, such as page views, country and browser type. It does not identify you personally.

Children

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out and no chat, and it collects no data, so it is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
